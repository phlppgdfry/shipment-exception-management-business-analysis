# 2-minute demo script

For a screen share at the start of an interview. Open the [control tower](https://phlppgdfry.github.io/shipment-exception-management-business-analysis/control-tower/) and press **Reset demo** first.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Overview, guided scenario step 1 | "Fictional operator. This reefer for a key account is on schedule — window Thursday early morning." |
| 0:15 | Step 2: **Publish the sailing delay** | "The sailing system says: six hours late." |
| 0:25 | Step 3 | "Eleven orders on that sailing. Only four become exceptions — the ones now expected *after* their window. That one rule removed most of the noise." |
| 0:45 | Open EX-1012 | "Critical: late, reefer one level up, key account so a call task too. Every decision shows its rule IDs." |
| 1:05 | Steps 5–6: assign, act | "The shift lead assigns; the planner calls the consignee and adds a note to the customer message." |
| 1:25 | Customer view | "The customer sees one clear message — new time, reason, next step. Nothing internal." |
| 1:40 | Step 8, then BA workspace → examples | "New window agreed, back on schedule, resolved with a reason. And these rules are examples that ran as tests in this build." |
| 2:00 | — | "Happy to go deeper into any step — the business case, the workshop or the architecture." |
