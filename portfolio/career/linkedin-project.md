**Shipment exception management — business analysis case (portfolio)**
Independent case for a fictional short-sea and door-to-door operator whose customers heard about delays at the gate. Problem framing and business case with a pilot and stage gate; event storming; BPMN 2.0 with timer events; 13 business rules as DMN and as Given/When/Then examples that run as tests; WSJF backlog; UX; acceptance testing, go/no-go and adoption plan; a working control tower demo. Simulated stakeholders and delivery; real tests. Built with AI assistance.
