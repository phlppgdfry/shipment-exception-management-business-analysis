# Recruiter view — 90 seconds

**Independent portfolio case · fictional organisation · simulated delivery · real tests and demo.**

**The challenge.** A short-sea and door-to-door operator's customers learned about late deliveries at the gate. Only 22% were told in time; "where is my load?" was 38% of all contacts. Three managers asked for three different solutions.

**What the analyst did.** Reframed the requests as one problem — no rules for what counts as late and who tells the customer — and took it from business case to go-live: discovery and event storming, BPMN, 13 rules with tests, a prioritised backlog with the Product Owner, screen design, acceptance testing, go/no-go and a measured pilot.
