# Hiring manager view

**Independent portfolio case — not a client engagement.** The strongest evidence is the reasoning between an ambiguous request and something a team can build, test and run.

## Ambiguity and stakeholder alignment

"A tracking page", "mails to key accounts" and "nothing that adds work" are three solutions. The [problem statement](../../docs/01-opportunity/problem-statement.md) finds the shared gap with five whys and names what would change the conclusion. The [workshop dialogue](../../docs/03-workshops/workshop-dialogue.md) shows how an account manager's "tell them everything" and a planner's "no message storm" became one concept — severity — and one decision owner ([D-02](../../docs/03-workshops/decision-log.md#d-02)). The analyst prepares options; the PO decides ([roles](../../docs/08-delivery/roles-and-responsibilities.md)).

## Trade-offs and value

Four options were scored on weights agreed **before** scoring ([business case](../../docs/01-opportunity/options-and-business-case.md)). The cheap option was tested on paper and rejected as noise. Because the case depends on one unknown — how many calls disappear — the sponsor funded a pilot with a control group and a stage gate ([D-07](../../docs/03-workshops/decision-log.md#d-07)). The illustrative readout adjusts the business case downwards instead of inflating it ([benefits review](../../docs/11-measure/benefits-review.md)).

## Precision that survives delivery

Thirteen rules in one table, also as [DMN](../../docs/05-requirements/dmn/exception-decisions.dmn), with 30 examples that run as tests. When the PO moves a threshold, the failing examples show what changes meaning. The [backlog](../../docs/06-backlog/prioritisation.md) is ordered by WSJF; dependencies sequence it; the [second iteration](../../docs/06-backlog/release-2-edi-status.md) shows a story that is honestly *not ready* yet.

## Owning the outcome, not just the documents

[BAT](../../docs/09-acceptance/bat-log.md) with key users — including the sceptic — found four majors; one came from the analyst's own refinement answer, and the retro action is recorded. [Go/no-go](../../docs/10-go-live/release-readiness.md) has an owner per line; [change and adoption](../../docs/10-go-live/change-and-adoption.md) measures whether people actually use it; the [runbook](../../docs/10-go-live/operations-runbook.md) says what support does when it breaks.

## Interview probes worth asking

- "Which finding could have changed your recommendation?" — the WISMO sample; the supplier roadmap.
- "Who decided the 2-hour threshold, and what did the planner say?" — PO; the 1-hour proposal would have created ±60% more exceptions.
- "Tell me about a mistake." — DEF-04.
- "What did building the demo teach you?" — five open questions (OQ-01 – OQ-05) and a privacy leak.
