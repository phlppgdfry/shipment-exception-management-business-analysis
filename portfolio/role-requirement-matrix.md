## Tailor for an actual vacancy

Take the employer's top three responsibilities verbatim, link the strongest evidence for each, and say the gap out loud before they find it. For analysis-heavy roles lead with the rules, traceability and BPMN; for delivery-heavy roles with BAT, go/no-go and adoption; for product roles with the business case and stage gate.

This matrix is not a certification or evidence of professional client experience. [Portfolio](README.md)
