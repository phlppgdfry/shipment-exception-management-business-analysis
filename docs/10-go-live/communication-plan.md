# Communication plan

> **What this is:** who hears what about release 1, when, from whom and through which channel, so that the feature is "understood by everyone involved", not just deployed. **For:** PO, Head of CS, road planning lead, account managers. **Previous:** [release readiness](release-readiness.md) · **Next:** [support handover](support-handover.md).

## Messages per audience

| Audience | What they need to know | What changes for them | When | Channel | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Road planners | The list replaces the disruption sheet for pilot customers; what severity means; hold for missing status; response times | New work list, no more manual lookups for pilot customers | week 5 (training), week 6 (Monday stand-up) | 2 × 30-min training per shift, quick guide, Monday huddle | road planning lead + BA |
| Shift leads | Escalations come to them; how to assign | New view | week 5 | 15 min with the planning lead | BA |
| Customer service | Pilot customers get messages; call tasks for critical key-account cases; what to answer when a customer asks "why did I get this?" | Call tasks; fewer WISMO calls (hopefully) | week 5 | team meeting + KB article | Head of CS + BA |
| Account managers | Which of their customers are in the pilot; key accounts get a call for critical cases; what they will **not** get (every minor change) | Fewer surprises; no manual e-mails for pilot customers | week 4 (after the review) | AM team meeting, 20 min | PO |
| Pilot customers | What they will receive, how often, from when, how to give feedback, how to opt in to every update | Proactive messages from Monday week 6 | week 5 (Tuesday) | letter by e-mail from their AM + 15-min call for those who want it | account manager |
| Customers not in the pilot | Nothing yet | — | — | — | — |
| Application support | What the feature does, how to answer "why", known issues, escalation to the team | New component to support | week 5 | 45-min handover + KB | BA + developer |
| Management team | Pilot started, what we measure, when the stage gate is | — | week 6 | one paragraph in the weekly operations update | PO |

## Pilot customer letter (draft, sent by the account manager)

> **Subject:** From Monday: we tell you first when a delivery will miss its window
>
> From Monday [date], you are one of five customers in a pilot of proactive delivery updates.
>
> **What you will receive:** an e-mail and a message in the customer portal when one of your door-to-door deliveries is expected **more than two hours after the end of the agreed delivery window**, or moves to another day, or its sailing is cancelled. For refrigerated or time-critical loads we tell you earlier. The message contains the new ETA, the reason and what we are doing about it.
>
> **What you will not receive:** a message for every status change. If the delivery is still inside your window, nothing changes for you. If you prefer to hear about every change, tell your account manager.
>
> **Your feedback** decides how we continue. We will ask you for 10 minutes after two weeks and after six weeks.
>
> Questions: your account manager or customer service, as usual.
